Brain fog isn't just a lack of coffee; it's a symptom of underlying physiological friction. When your brain isn't getting the right fuel, hydration, or rest, it slows down to conserve energy.

## The Hydration Connection

Your brain is 73% water. Even a 2% drop in hydration can lead to noticeable cognitive decline, difficulty focusing, and that cloudy feeling.

<KeyTakeaways>
- **Front-load water:** Drink 16-20oz of water immediately upon waking before any caffeine.
- **Electrolytes matter:** Water alone isn't enough; add a pinch of sea salt or an electrolyte powder to ensure cellular hydration.
</KeyTakeaways>

## Blood Sugar Rollercoasters

If you eat a heavy carbohydrate breakfast (like cereal or pastries), your blood sugar spikes and then crashes. That crash is brain fog.

<CoachTip>
Switch to a savory, protein-heavy breakfast. Think eggs, avocado, and spinach. Stabilizing your morning blood sugar sets the tone for all-day focus.
</CoachTip>

<FAQ>
**Q: Can stress cause brain fog?**
A: Absolutely. Chronic stress keeps cortisol elevated, which over time can impair the prefrontal cortex, the part of the brain responsible for focus and decision-making.
</FAQ>
