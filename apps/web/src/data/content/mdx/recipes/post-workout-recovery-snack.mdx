# The Ultimate Post-Workout Recovery Snack

You just crushed your workout. Your muscles are tired, your glycogen stores are depleted, and your body is primed to absorb nutrients. What you eat next determines how well you recover, build muscle, and bounce back for your next session.

You need two things: **Protein** (to repair muscle tissue) and **Carbohydrates** (to replenish energy). This simple, delicious smoothie delivers both perfectly.

<KeyTakeaways>
- Consuming protein within 60 minutes of your workout maximizes muscle repair.
- Carbohydrates are essential post-workout to shuttle the protein into your muscles.
- This smoothie tastes like a milkshake but is packed with recovery nutrients.
</KeyTakeaways>

## The "Chocolate Banana Builder" Smoothie

### Ingredients
- **1 Scoop Chocolate Protein Powder** (Whey or Plant-based, ~25g protein)
- **1 Large Ripe Banana** (Frozen is best for a creamy texture)
- **1 Tablespoon Peanut Butter** (For flavor and healthy fats)
- **1 Cup Almond Milk or Fairlife Milk** (Use Fairlife for extra protein)
- **A Pinch of Sea Salt** (Crucial for replenishing electrolytes lost through sweat)
- **Ice cubes**

### Instructions
1. **Blend**: Toss all ingredients into a blender.
2. **Mix**: Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth. 
3. **Drink**: Enjoy immediately while your body is eager for nutrients!

<CoachTip>
If you had a particularly sweaty session, don't skip the sea salt! It dramatically improves the flavor by balancing the sweetness and helps replace the sodium you just sweat out.
</CoachTip>

## Why Carbs Matter Post-Workout

Many people try to avoid carbs after a workout, especially if their goal is weight loss. But this is a mistake! When you exercise, you burn through your muscle's stored carbohydrates (glycogen). Eating carbs like a banana causes a small insulin spike. This insulin acts like a key, opening up your muscle cells so that the protein can get inside and start repairing the damage.
