# 15-Minute High-Protein Breakfast

Mornings are chaotic. Between getting dressed, checking emails, and rushing out the door, breakfast often becomes an afterthought. But grabbing a sugary pastry or skipping breakfast entirely is a fast track to brain fog and mid-morning crashes. 

This 15-minute high-protein bowl changes the game. It’s savory, deeply satisfying, and packs over 30 grams of protein to kickstart your metabolism and stabilize your blood sugar.

<KeyTakeaways>
- This recipe provides 30g+ of high-quality protein to support muscle health and satiety.
- Takes less than 15 minutes from prep to plate.
- Highly customizable—use whatever veggies you have in the fridge!
</KeyTakeaways>

## Ingredients You Need

- **3 Large Eggs**: The gold standard of bioavailable protein.
- **1/2 Cup Cottage Cheese**: The secret ingredient! It makes the eggs incredibly fluffy and doubles the protein content.
- **1 Handful of Spinach**: For a quick hit of vitamins and fiber.
- **1 Slice of Sourdough Bread**: A gut-friendly carbohydrate for immediate energy.
- **1/4 Avocado**: For healthy fats that keep you full.
- **Salt, pepper, and a dash of hot sauce** (optional).

## Instructions

1. **Prep the Eggs**: In a bowl, crack the eggs and add the cottage cheese. Whisk vigorously until mostly smooth (a few curds are fine, they will melt into the eggs). 
2. **Toast the Bread**: Pop your sourdough into the toaster.
3. **Scramble**: Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat with a tiny bit of butter or olive oil. Pour in the egg mixture. 
4. **Add Greens**: As the eggs start to set, toss in the spinach and gently fold the eggs until cooked to your liking. 
5. **Assemble**: Mash the avocado onto your toasted sourdough. Top with the fluffy, cheesy scrambled eggs. Add salt, pepper, and hot sauce.

<CoachTip>
Don't fear the cottage cheese! Even if you aren't a fan of its texture normally, when whisked into eggs and cooked, it melts completely and just leaves behind an incredibly rich, creamy texture and a huge protein boost.
</CoachTip>

## Why This Breakfast Works

Starting your day with protein sets the tone for your hormones. It suppresses ghrelin (the hunger hormone) and boosts peptide YY (the hormone that makes you feel full).
