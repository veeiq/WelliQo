For high-performers, stress is a constant companion. But unmanaged chronic stress destroys your metabolic health, sleep, and longevity. This guide offers practical tools to down-regulate your nervous system amidst chaos.

## The Physiology of Stress

When you're under pressure, your sympathetic nervous system (fight or flight) is hyperactive. We need to intentionally activate the parasympathetic nervous system (rest and digest).

<KeyTakeaways>
- **Breath is the remote control:** How you breathe directly dictates your nervous system state.
- **Micro-breaks matter:** 2 minutes of reset is better than zero.
- **Boundaries are biological:** Mental boundaries protect physical health.
</KeyTakeaways>

## The Physiological Sigh

This is the fastest, scientifically proven way to calm down in real-time.

<CoachTip>
Take two quick inhales through the nose, followed by a long, slow exhale through the mouth. Repeat 3 times before your next high-stakes meeting.
</CoachTip>

## Structuring Decompression

You cannot run at 100% all day and expect to instantly fall asleep. You need a buffer zone.

<FAQ>
**Q: I don't have time to meditate. What else can I do?**
A: You don't need a 30-minute sit. Even a 5-minute walk without your phone, focusing on the horizon, can shift your nervous system out of high gear.
</FAQ>
