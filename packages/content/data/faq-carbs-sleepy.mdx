# Why do carbs make me sleepy?

**User Question:**

> "Whenever I eat pasta or rice for lunch, I feel exhausted an hour later. What is happening?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Large amounts of carbohydrates trigger a massive release of insulin to clear the glucose from your blood. This insulin surge also clears out competing amino acids, allowing an amino acid called tryptophan to cross the blood-brain barrier easily. Tryptophan is a precursor to serotonin and melatonin (the sleep hormone). You are literally creating a sleep potion in your brain.
