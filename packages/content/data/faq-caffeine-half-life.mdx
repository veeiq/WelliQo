# How long does caffeine stay in my system?

**User Question:**

> "If I drink a coffee at 4 PM, will it affect my sleep at 11 PM?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Absolutely. Caffeine has a half-life of roughly 5 to 6 hours, and a quarter-life of 10 to 12 hours. If you consume 200mg of caffeine at 4 PM, you still have 100mg active in your brain at 9 PM, and 50mg active at 2 AM. This ruins the architecture of your deep sleep, even if you don't realize it.
