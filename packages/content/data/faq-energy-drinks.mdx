# Are zero-sugar energy drinks healthy?

**User Question:**

> "I switched from soda to zero-sugar energy drinks. Is this better for my energy?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
They prevent the immediate blood sugar spike, which is an improvement. However, they often contain massive, unregulated doses of synthetic caffeine (up to 300mg) and artificial sweeteners that can disrupt your gut microbiome. Chronic use leads to adrenal fatigue. Opt for controlled, cleaner sources like Matcha or a Liftoff Mocktail instead.
