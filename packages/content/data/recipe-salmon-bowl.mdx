# Omega-3 Salmon & Quinoa Bowl

**Purpose:** To provide a nutrient-dense lunch that prevents the 3 PM crash by prioritizing high-quality fats and complex carbohydrates.
**Best Timing:** Lunch (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM).
**Target Audience:** Professionals seeking sustained afternoon cognitive performance without feeling sluggish.

## Why This Recipe Works

A standard lunch of sandwiches or pasta causes a massive insulin spike, leading directly to the 3 PM crash. This bowl replaces refined carbs with quinoa (a complex, slow-digesting seed) and adds wild-caught salmon. Salmon is rich in Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA), which are critical for reducing inflammation and supporting mitochondrial health (your cellular energy factories). 

## Nutrition Highlights
- **Protein:** 35g
- **Healthy Fats:** 22g (High in Omega-3s)
- **Carbohydrates:** 30g (Complex, high fiber)
- **Key Micronutrients:** Magnesium, Vitamin B12, Iron

## Ingredients

- 1 filet (5-6 oz) wild-caught salmon
- 1/2 cup cooked quinoa
- 1 cup mixed greens or arugula
- 1/4 avocado, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Sea salt and black pepper to taste

## Preparation (20 Minutes)

1. **Cook Quinoa:** If not pre-cooked, boil quinoa according to package instructions (usually 1 part quinoa to 2 parts water for 15 minutes).
2. **Sear Salmon:** Heat a pan over medium-high heat with a dash of olive oil. Season the salmon with salt and pepper. Cook for 4 minutes skin-side down, flip, and cook for 2 more minutes.
3. **Assemble:** Place the mixed greens at the bottom of a bowl. Add the cooked quinoa, sliced avocado, and the seared salmon on top.
4. **Dress:** Drizzle with olive oil and fresh lemon juice.

## Related Habits
Always follow this lunch with a [10-Minute Post-Meal Walk](/action-hub/post-meal-walk) to ensure the carbohydrates from the quinoa are absorbed by your muscles rather than spiking your blood sugar.
