# Does black coffee break my morning fast?

**User Question:**

> "I practice intermittent fasting to keep my energy stable. Does black coffee break a fast?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
No, black coffee does not break a fast from a metabolic perspective. It contains near-zero calories and will not trigger an insulin response. In fact, caffeine can mildly enhance fat oxidation during a fast. However, if you add milk, sugar, collagen, or butter, you will break the fast and trigger digestion, which redirects energy away from your brain and towards your stomach.
