# 2-Minute Morning Energy Shake

Healthy food is always the best foundation, but some mornings you simply don't have 15 minutes to cook eggs.

Skipping breakfast or grabbing a pastry will cause a massive energy crash by mid-morning. This shake is a perfectly balanced accelerator for those busy days. It provides the protein you need to stabilize blood sugar in under two minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops Herbalife Formula 1 (Vanilla or Chocolate)
- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk (or water)
- 1/2 banana (optional, for creaminess)
- Handful of ice

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
2. Blend for 30 seconds until smooth.

**Why it works:** You secure a complete, balanced meal with high-quality soy protein, preventing the blood sugar rollercoaster that leads to afternoon fatigue.
