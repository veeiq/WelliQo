# 2-Minute Morning Energy Shake

**Purpose:** To provide a complete, macro-balanced meal in under two minutes for days when cooking is impossible.
**Best Timing:** Within 30 to 60 minutes of waking up.
**Target Audience:** Busy professionals and parents who often skip breakfast due to time constraints, leading to severe afternoon fatigue.

## Why This Recipe Works

Skipping breakfast when you are stressed elevates cortisol and adrenaline, making you feel anxious rather than energized. This shake uses Herbalife Formula 1 as a nutritional accelerator, providing a clinically tested balance of protein, complex carbohydrates, and 21 essential vitamins and minerals. It stabilizes blood sugar immediately, preventing the midday crash.

## Nutrition Highlights

- **Protein:** 24g (when made with soy or dairy milk)
- **Carbohydrates:** 21g (complex)
- **Fiber:** 3g
- **Key Micronutrients:** Vitamin C, E, and B-complex vitamins for cellular energy.

## Ingredients

- 2 scoops (25g) Herbalife Formula 1 Healthy Meal Nutritional Shake Mix (Vanilla or Chocolate)
- 8 fl oz of unsweetened soy milk or low-fat dairy milk (for protein content)
- 1/2 cup of ice cubes
- _Optional:_ 1 handful of frozen spinach (adds micronutrients without changing the taste)
- _Optional:_ 1 tbsp chia seeds (for extra fiber and Omega-3s)

## Preparation (2 Minutes)

1. **Liquid First:** Pour the milk into a blender. Adding liquid first prevents the powder from sticking to the bottom.
2. **Add Ingredients:** Add the Formula 1 powder, ice, and any optional add-ins.
3. **Blend:** Blend on high for 30 to 45 seconds until completely smooth and creamy.
4. **Serve:** Pour into a travel cup and drink within 30 minutes.
