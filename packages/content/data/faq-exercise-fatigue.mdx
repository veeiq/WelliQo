# I feel too tired to exercise. What should I do?

**User Question:**

> "I know exercise gives you energy, but I am too exhausted after work to go to the gym. How do I break the cycle?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
You are stuck in a negative feedback loop: you don't move because you're tired, but you're tired because you don't move. Break the cycle by lowering the barrier to entry. Do not try to do a 60-minute gym session. Commit to a 10-minute walk outside or 5 minutes of stretching. Often, the hardest part is simply starting, and the light movement will generate the energy you need.
