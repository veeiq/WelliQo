# How do I start improving my sleep?

**User Question:**
> "I want better sleep and more energy. Where do I start?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Sleep is the ultimate performance enhancer. It is when your body repairs muscle, consolidates memory, and resets your hormones for the next day. 

The easiest place to start is your light environment. Focus on getting sunlight in your eyes within 30 minutes of waking, and implement a digital sunset by turning off all screens 90 minutes before bed. This regulates your circadian rhythm naturally without the need for supplements.
