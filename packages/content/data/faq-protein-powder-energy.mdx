# Does protein powder give you energy?

**User Question:**
> "If I add protein powder to my morning smoothie, will it give me an energy boost?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Protein doesn't give you a sudden 'jolt' of energy like caffeine. Instead, it slows down the digestion of the entire meal, ensuring that the carbohydrates in your smoothie enter your bloodstream slowly. This prevents a rapid spike and crash, giving you sustained, reliable energy for hours.
