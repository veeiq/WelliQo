# How much water do I actually need for energy?

**User Question:**
> "I know I should drink water, but how much do I actually need to prevent fatigue?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Even a 2% drop in hydration can lead to a 20% drop in cognitive performance and noticeable fatigue. A practical rule of thumb is to drink half your body weight in ounces per day as a baseline. For example, if you weigh 160 lbs, aim for 80 oz of water. If you are drinking plenty of water but still feel thirsty or tired, you likely need electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) to help your cells absorb it.
