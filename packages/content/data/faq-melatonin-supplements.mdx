# Should I take melatonin supplements for better energy?

**User Question:**

> "I wake up tired because I sleep poorly. Should I take melatonin pills?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Proceed with caution. Most over-the-counter melatonin supplements contain massive doses (5mg to 10mg), which is up to 30 times more than your brain naturally produces. This can cause severe grogginess the next day. It is far better to optimize your body's natural melatonin production by viewing morning sunlight and avoiding screens 90 minutes before bed.
