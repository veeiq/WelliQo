# 15-Minute Protein Scramble

A high-protein breakfast stabilizes your blood sugar. If you usually eat toast or cereal, your blood sugar crashes by 10:30 AM, causing intense fatigue and cravings. This simple scramble prevents the crash.

## Ingredients

- 3 whole eggs
- 1 cup spinach
- 1/4 cup cottage cheese or feta
- Pinch of salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs in a bowl.
2. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a dash of olive oil.
3. Toss in the spinach until slightly wilted.
4. Pour in the eggs and gently scramble.
5. Fold in the cheese right before removing from heat.

**Why it works:** You get ~25g of high-quality protein and essential healthy fats to keep you satiated and energized until lunch.
