# 15-Minute Protein Scramble

**Purpose:** To stabilize morning blood sugar, prevent the 11 AM crash, and provide sustained energy through high-quality protein and healthy fats.
**Best Timing:** Within 30 to 60 minutes of waking up.
**Target Audience:** Working professionals and parents who need rapid, reliable morning fuel without a mid-morning crash.

## Why This Recipe Works

A standard breakfast of cereal or toast spikes your blood sugar and insulin, leading to a severe energy crash. This scramble provides **32 grams of protein**, which digests slowly, keeps you full until lunch, and blunts any glucose spikes. The yolks provide choline, an essential nutrient for brain health and focus.

## Nutrition Highlights

- **Protein:** 32g
- **Carbohydrates:** 4g
- **Healthy Fats:** 18g
- **Key Micronutrients:** Choline, Vitamin D, B12

## Ingredients

- 3 whole, pasture-raised eggs
- 1/2 cup liquid egg whites (for extra, low-fat protein)
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 1/4 avocado, sliced
- 1 tsp grass-fed butter or olive oil
- A pinch of high-quality sea salt and black pepper

## Preparation (15 Minutes)

1. **Prep:** In a bowl, whisk the whole eggs and egg whites together.
2. **Heat:** Melt the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat.
3. **Cook Greens:** Toss in the spinach and cook for 30 seconds until slightly wilted.
4. **Scramble:** Pour the egg mixture into the pan. Stir slowly with a spatula, pushing the eggs from the edges to the center until they form soft curds.
5. **Serve:** Remove from heat _just_ before they look completely done (they will continue cooking on the plate). Top with sliced avocado, sea salt, and black pepper.
