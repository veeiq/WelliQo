# Why do I wake up tired after 8 hours of sleep?

**User Question:**
> "I am in bed for a full 8 hours, but I still wake up exhausted. Why?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Being in bed is not the same as being asleep, and being asleep is not the same as getting deep, restorative sleep. If your room is too warm, if you drank alcohol before bed, or if you had caffeine late in the day, your brain may spend the entire night in light sleep stages, never reaching the deep sleep required for physical recovery.
