# Why am I always tired?

If you wake up feeling like you haven't slept, the problem usually isn't your mattress. It's how your body understands time.

Your body relies on external cues to know when to be alert and when to rest. When those cues are missing, you exist in a permanent state of "jet lag" without ever leaving your house.

This simple protocol focuses on the first 30 minutes of your day to reset your internal clock and give you sustained energy without relying entirely on caffeine.

## The ONE next step

Tomorrow morning, within 30 minutes of waking up, step outside for 5 minutes. That's it. Do not look at your phone first. Let the natural light hit your eyes.

When you are ready to build on this, explore the habits and breakfast recipes connected to this guide.
