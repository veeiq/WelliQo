# How long should a power nap be?

**User Question:**
> "If I am exhausted, is it okay to take a nap? How long should it be?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Yes, but timing is everything. A power nap should be exactly 20 to 25 minutes. This is long enough to clear out some adenosine (sleep pressure) but short enough that you wake up before entering deep sleep. If you sleep for 45 to 60 minutes, you will wake up in the middle of a deep sleep cycle, resulting in severe 'sleep inertia' and feeling much worse than before.
