# Can I catch up on sleep on the weekends?

**User Question:**

> "I sleep 5 hours a night during the week. Can I just sleep 10 hours on the weekend to catch up?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Unfortunately, no. Sleep is not a bank account; you cannot pay off a biological debt in a single weekend. Furthermore, sleeping in on Saturday and Sunday severely disrupts your circadian rhythm, creating 'social jetlag.' This guarantees that you will struggle to fall asleep on Sunday night and start Monday morning exhausted.
