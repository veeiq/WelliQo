# Overnight Chia Pudding for Sustained Fuel

**Purpose:** A zero-prep morning meal that provides massive amounts of soluble fiber to stabilize blood sugar and prevent afternoon fatigue.
**Best Timing:** Breakfast or a mid-morning snack.
**Target Audience:** Parents and professionals whose mornings are too chaotic to cook, but who need sustained energy.

## Why This Recipe Works

Chia seeds are a nutritional powerhouse. When soaked in liquid, they absorb up to 10 times their weight, forming a gel-like consistency. This soluble fiber slows down digestion exponentially. Instead of a rapid glucose spike, the carbohydrates and nutrients are released in a slow, steady trickle into your bloodstream. This provides reliable energy for hours and completely prevents the mid-day crash.

## Nutrition Highlights
- **Fiber:** 10g (excellent for gut health and blood sugar control)
- **Healthy Fats:** 9g (rich in plant-based Omega-3s)
- **Protein:** 6g (can be boosted)

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp chia seeds
- 3/4 cup unsweetened almond milk or coconut milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup fresh berries (blueberries or raspberries)
- *Optional:* 1 scoop of protein powder to increase satiety

## Preparation (5 Minutes prep, overnight chill)

1. **Mix:** In a mason jar or small container, combine the chia seeds, milk, and vanilla extract. 
2. **Stir:** Stir thoroughly. Wait 5 minutes, then stir again. (This prevents the seeds from clumping at the bottom).
3. **Chill:** Cover the jar and place it in the refrigerator overnight (or for at least 2 hours).
4. **Serve:** In the morning, the mixture will be thick and creamy. Top with fresh berries before eating.
