# 5 Minutes of Morning Sunlight

The most powerful energy drink on earth is free, and it's available every morning.

Viewing sunlight within 30-60 minutes of waking up triggers a healthy spike in cortisol. This doesn't just wake you up right now—it actually starts a 14-hour timer that helps you fall asleep faster tonight.

## How to do it:
1. Wake up and go outside (do not look through a window; glass filters the necessary light).
2. Look toward the sun (but never directly at it).
3. Stay outside for 5-10 minutes. If it's cloudy, stay out for 15-20 minutes.

**Why it works:** It signals to your brain that the day has officially begun, halting the production of melatonin (the sleep hormone).
