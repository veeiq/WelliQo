# 30g Protein Within 30 Minutes of Waking

The traditional modern breakfast—cereal, toast, bagels, or pastries—is an energy disaster. It spikes your blood sugar immediately, guaranteeing a crash before noon. To sustain energy all day, you must change what you eat first.

## The Protocol

**Consume at least 30 grams of high-quality protein within 30 to 60 minutes of waking up.**

## Why This Works

Your first meal of the day sets your blood sugar baseline. If you eat a meal high in refined carbohydrates and low in protein, your glucose spikes rapidly. Your body overcompensates by releasing too much insulin, leading to a blood sugar crash two hours later. This crash causes mid-morning fatigue, brain fog, and intense cravings for more sugar.

Protein, on the other hand, is highly satiating and digests slowly. Consuming 30 grams of protein first thing in the morning accomplishes three things:

1. **Stabilizes Blood Sugar:** It flattens your glucose curve, providing a steady drip of energy rather than a spike and crash.
2. **Hormonal Regulation:** It blunts the hunger hormone (ghrelin) and boosts satiety hormones (leptin), keeping you full until lunch.
3. **Dopamine Production:** Protein breaks down into amino acids like tyrosine, which are the direct building blocks for dopamine—the neurotransmitter responsible for motivation and focus.

## How to Get 30 Grams

Achieving 30 grams requires intentionality. Here are a few examples:

- 4 whole eggs (24g) + 1 oz of cheese (7g)
- 1 cup of Greek yogurt (20g) + a scoop of collagen peptides (10g)
- A high-quality [Morning Energy Shake](/action-hub/2-minute-morning-energy-shake) (24g+)

Do not wait until lunch to prioritize protein. Front-load it, and your energy will remain stable all day.
