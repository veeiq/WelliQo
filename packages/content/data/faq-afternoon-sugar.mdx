# Why do I crave sugar specifically at 3 PM?

**User Question:**

> "Every afternoon, I get an overwhelming craving for chocolate or soda. Why?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
This is a biological alarm bell, not a lack of willpower. If your lunch was carbohydrate-heavy, your blood sugar spiked and is now crashing. Your brain interprets this rapid drop in glucose as a survival threat and demands the fastest source of fuel available: simple sugar. Prevent this by walking for 10 minutes after lunch and prioritizing protein.
