# Herbalife Aloe Soothing Drink

**Purpose:** To support healthy digestion and enhance nutrient absorption, indirectly boosting overall daily energy.
**Best Timing:** First thing in the morning, or immediately before a large meal.
**Target Audience:** Individuals who experience bloating or sluggishness after eating.

## Why This Recipe Works

Energy is not just about what you eat; it is about what you _absorb_. If your digestive system is inflamed or sluggish, your body expends massive amounts of energy trying to process food, leaving you feeling lethargic.

Aloe vera has been used for centuries to soothe the stomach and support nutrient absorption. This drink uses Herbalife Herbal Aloe Concentrate to provide the soothing benefits of aloe in a highly purified, great-tasting format, preparing your gut to extract maximum energy from your meals.

## Nutrition Highlights

- **Calories:** 0 (will not break a fast)
- **Key Benefits:** Soothes the stomach, supports healthy digestion, encourages daily hydration.

## Ingredients

- 3 capfuls (15 ml) Herbalife Herbal Aloe Concentrate (Mango or Mandarin flavor)
- 16 oz (about 500 ml) of filtered water (room temperature or cold)
- _Optional:_ A slice of fresh lemon or cucumber
- _Optional:_ A pinch of sea salt for electrolytes

## Preparation (2 Minutes)

1. **Measure:** Pour 16 ounces of water into a large glass or water bottle.
2. **Mix:** Add 3 capfuls of Herbal Aloe Concentrate.
3. **Enhance:** Add the optional slice of lemon or a tiny pinch of sea salt.
4. **Stir:** Stir gently and drink immediately.

## Related Habits

Use this drink to upgrade your [Morning Hydration with Sea Salt](/action-hub/morning-hydration-salt) habit. It makes drinking 16 ounces of water first thing in the morning delicious and effortless.
