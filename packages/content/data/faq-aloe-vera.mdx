# How does Aloe Vera support daily energy?

**User Question:**
> "I've heard drinking aloe vera helps with energy. Is that true?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Aloe vera does not contain caffeine or calories, so it does not provide direct energy. However, it profoundly soothes the digestive tract and reduces gut inflammation. When your gut is inflamed, your body expends massive amounts of energy trying to heal and digest. By soothing the gut, aloe vera frees up systemic energy and improves nutrient absorption.
