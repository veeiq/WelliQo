# Golden Milk for Evening Recovery

**Purpose:** To reduce physical inflammation and signal to the brain that it is time to down-regulate and prepare for deep sleep.
**Best Timing:** 60 to 90 minutes before bed.
**Target Audience:** Anyone who struggles to "switch off" their brain at night or experiences physical stiffness.

## Why This Recipe Works

Tomorrow's energy is determined by tonight's recovery. If you go to bed inflamed or stressed, your sleep architecture will be poor, and you will wake up exhausted. 

Golden Milk (Haldi Doodh) is a traditional Ayurvedic beverage. Its primary ingredient, turmeric, contains **curcumin**, a highly potent anti-inflammatory compound. Warm liquids also naturally raise your core body temperature slightly; as your body cools down afterward, it triggers a steep drop in temperature that initiates the release of melatonin.

## Nutrition Highlights
- **Key Compounds:** Curcumin (anti-inflammatory), Piperine (enhances curcumin absorption by 2000%)
- **Caffeine:** 0g

## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsweetened almond milk or oat milk
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- A tiny pinch of black pepper (Crucial: Piperine from black pepper is required to absorb the turmeric)
- 1/2 tsp coconut oil or ghee (Curcumin is fat-soluble)
- *Optional:* 1/2 tsp raw honey or monk fruit sweetener

## Preparation (10 Minutes)

1. **Heat:** Pour the milk into a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Do not let it boil.
2. **Whisk:** Add the turmeric, cinnamon, black pepper, and coconut oil. Whisk continuously until the spices are fully dissolved and the milk turns a rich golden color.
3. **Simmer:** Let it simmer gently for 5 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.
4. **Serve:** Pour into a mug, stir in the honey (if using), and sip slowly.
