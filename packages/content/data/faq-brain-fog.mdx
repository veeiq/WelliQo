# What exactly is brain fog?

**User Question:**

> "People talk about 'brain fog' all the time. What is actually happening biologically?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Brain fog is a symptom of neuroinflammation or a lack of adequate fuel delivery to the brain. It can be caused by dehydration (which thickens the blood), poor sleep (which prevents the brain from clearing metabolic waste), or high blood sugar variability. It is your brain's way of signaling that its basic operating requirements are not being met.
