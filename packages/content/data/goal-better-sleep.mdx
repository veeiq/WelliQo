# Goal: Better Sleep & More Energy

Sleep is the ultimate performance enhancer. It is when your body repairs muscle, consolidates memory, and resets your hormones for the next day.

This goal focuses on improving the depth and consistency of your sleep, so you can wake up with natural energy.

Progress toward this goal is measured by how you feel at 2 PM, and how quickly you fall asleep at 10 PM.
