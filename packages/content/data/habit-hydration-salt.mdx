# Morning Hydration with Sea Salt

Most people wake up, stumble into the kitchen, and immediately pour a cup of coffee. This is a critical mistake for your daily energy. Before you stimulate your nervous system with caffeine, you must rehydrate your cells.

## The Protocol

**Within 10 minutes of waking up, drink 16 ounces (about 500ml) of room-temperature water with a small pinch of high-quality sea salt.**

## Why This Works

While you sleep, you exhale nearly a liter of water vapor. You wake up in a state of mild, chronic dehydration. Dehydration thickens your blood, forcing your heart to work harder to pump oxygen to your brain. This lack of oxygen is a primary driver of morning brain fog.

Drinking plain water is good, but adding a tiny pinch of unrefined sea salt (like Celtic or Himalayan salt) is vastly superior. 

Here is the science: Water follows sodium. If you drink a massive amount of plain, filtered water, it often runs straight through your digestive system, leading to immediate trips to the bathroom. The trace minerals and electrolytes in sea salt allow your body to actually pull that water into your cells, hydrating you at a cellular level instantly.

## The Execution
- Keep a glass of water and a small container of salt on your nightstand.
- Drink it before you check your phone.
- Do not use standard white table salt (which is stripped of trace minerals and often contains anti-caking agents). Use high-quality, unrefined salt.

> [!TIP]
> **Temperature Matters**
> Room temperature water is easiest on your digestive system first thing in the morning. Ice cold water forces your body to expend energy to warm it up before it can be absorbed.
