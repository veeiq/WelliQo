# Liftoff Afternoon Energy Mocktail

**Purpose:** To provide a crisp, refreshing, and highly controlled energy boost to conquer the 3 PM crash without ruining your sleep.
**Best Timing:** 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM (Do not consume after 4:00 PM).
**Target Audience:** Professionals and students who need intense focus for the afternoon but want to avoid sugary energy drinks or heavy coffees.

## Why This Recipe Works

When the 3 PM crash hits, reaching for a sugary, 300mg-caffeine energy drink or a caramel latte is a mistake. The sugar will cause a secondary crash by 5 PM, and the massive dose of caffeine will still be in your bloodstream at midnight, destroying your deep sleep.

This mocktail uses a Herbalife Liftoff effervescent tablet. It provides exactly 75mg of caffeine (enough to increase alertness without overstimulation) and is packed with B-vitamins and Vitamin C to support your body's natural energy-yielding metabolism. Because it is effervescent, it hydrates you simultaneously.

## Nutrition Highlights

- **Caffeine:** 75mg (from Guarana and caffeine powder)
- **Sugar:** 0g
- **Key Micronutrients:** Vitamin C, Vitamin B6, Vitamin B12, Panthothenic Acid

## Ingredients

- 1 Herbalife Liftoff Tablet (Pomegranate-Berry or Lemon-Lime)
- 8-12 oz of cold, sparkling mineral water (or still water)
- 1 sprig of fresh mint
- 1/4 cup of ice
- _Optional:_ 1 capful of Herbal Aloe Concentrate for added flavor and digestive support

## Preparation (2 Minutes)

1. **Ice & Mint:** Place the ice and the sprig of fresh mint at the bottom of a glass.
2. **Pour:** Fill the glass with sparkling or still water.
3. **Drop:** Drop the Liftoff tablet into the water. Allow it to dissolve completely (the effervescence is part of the experience).
4. **Stir:** Stir gently and enjoy immediately.
