# L-Theanine Rich Matcha Latte

**Purpose:** To provide a focused, calm energy boost without the adrenaline spike and subsequent crash associated with coffee.
**Best Timing:** Mid-morning (around 10:00 AM) or early afternoon (before 2:00 PM).
**Target Audience:** Individuals seeking to reduce their coffee dependency or those who experience anxiety and jitters from high-caffeine beverages.

## Why This Recipe Works

Coffee delivers a harsh spike of caffeine that often triggers cortisol and adrenaline release. Matcha, which is made from ground whole green tea leaves, contains caffeine but pairs it naturally with an amino acid called **L-Theanine**. 

L-Theanine crosses the blood-brain barrier and increases alpha wave activity in the brain, promoting a state of "calm alertness." The caffeine is released slowly over 4-6 hours, completely eliminating the crash.

## Nutrition Highlights
- **Caffeine:** ~70mg (compared to ~150mg in a large coffee)
- **Key Compounds:** L-Theanine, EGCG (a powerful antioxidant that supports metabolism)

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp high-quality ceremonial grade matcha powder
- 2 oz hot water (not boiling; about 175°F/80°C)
- 6 oz unsweetened almond, oat, or soy milk
- *Optional:* 1 tsp honey or monk fruit sweetener

## Preparation (5 Minutes)

1. **Sift:** Sift the matcha powder into a wide mug or bowl to prevent clumps.
2. **Whisk:** Pour the hot water over the matcha. Using a bamboo whisk (chasen) or a small milk frother, whisk vigorously in a "W" or "M" motion until a fine froth appears on the surface.
3. **Warm the Milk:** Heat your milk of choice in a small saucepan or milk frother until warm.
4. **Combine:** Pour the warm milk over the frothed matcha. Sweeten to taste if desired.
