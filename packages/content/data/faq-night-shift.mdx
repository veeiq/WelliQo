# How do I manage energy if I work night shifts?

**User Question:**
> "My schedule constantly changes because of shift work. How can I manage my energy?"

**WelliQo Coaching Answer:**
Shift work forces you to fight your biology. To minimize the damage, control your light exposure meticulously. When you finish a night shift and drive home in the morning, wear dark sunglasses to trick your brain into thinking it is dusk. Keep your bedroom pitch black and cold to simulate nighttime while you sleep during the day.
