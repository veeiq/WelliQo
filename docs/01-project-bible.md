# WelliQo Project Bible

**Status:** Foundation draft  
**Owner:** WelliQo / VeeiQ Technologies  
**Decision rule:** Where business growth conflicts with policy, consumer safety, or truthfulness, choose policy, safety, and truthfulness.

## 1. Mission and scope

WelliQo will be a clear, useful wellness knowledge platform. It will help visitors explore balanced nutrition, everyday healthy habits, recipes, product education where permitted, and ways to request contact from an appropriately identified independent distributor.

The platform is not a medical service, a treatment provider, a replacement for professional advice, or an official Herbalife corporate property. It will not present itself as any of those things.

## 2. Outcomes

1. Answer real questions with evidence-aware, readable content.
2. Let visitors move naturally from learning to a relevant next action.
3. Capture only consented, necessary lead data.
4. Give editors a repeatable way to publish compliant, useful pages.
5. Build a technical base that remains fast and maintainable as the library grows.

## 3. Audience

| Audience                     | Primary need                                    | Appropriate next step                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wellness learner             | Practical, non-medical education                | Read a guide or save a recipe                                    |
| Existing customer            | Approved usage and preparation help             | Read approved product guidance or contact distributor            |
| Weight-management researcher | Realistic habit and nutrition guidance          | Use a general wellness tool or speak to a qualified professional |
| Prospective distributor      | Factual, policy-aligned opportunity information | Request an information conversation                              |

## 4. Product principles

- **Compliance first:** Content and functionality must pass the rules in the Compliance Manual.
- **Education first:** Pages answer the question before offering a call to action.
- **Evidence-aware:** Do not make health, earnings, lifestyle, or product claims without explicit approval and substantiation.
- **Privacy by design:** Collect minimal data, clearly explain use, and obtain required consent.
- **Accessible by default:** Keyboard operation, readable contrast, semantic structure, and responsive layouts are baseline requirements.
- **SEO serves people:** Search optimization must never distort intent or create thin, duplicate, or misleading pages.

## 5. Initial product pillars

1. **Guides:** nutrition, wellness routines, and product education where approved.
2. **Recipes:** clearly labelled preparation and nutrition context; no implied therapeutic results.
3. **Wellness tools:** educational questionnaires/calculators with transparent assumptions and disclaimers; never diagnostic.
4. **Distributor contact:** a consent-based form with clear independent-distributor identity.
5. **Resource library:** FAQs, glossary, and downloadable approved materials.

## 6. Guardrails

- Do not present WelliQo as Herbalife, its agent, or an official channel.
- Do not diagnose, cure, prevent, or treat disease.
- Do not guarantee weight, health, income, rank, lifestyle, or business outcomes.
- Do not publish unapproved imagery, trademark use, pricing, promotions, testimonials, or before/after material.
- Do not automate individualized health or business advice.
- Do not expose leads to unconsented outreach or sell personal data.

## 7. Success measures

| Area        | Measure                                                 | Quality constraint                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Helpfulness | Organic guide engagement, return visits, FAQ resolution | No deceptive SEO practices                              |
| Trust       | Consent rate, complaint rate, content-review pass rate  | Consent must be informed and freely given               |
| Performance | Core Web Vitals and accessible page completion          | Never trade accessibility for speed                     |
| Business    | Qualified, consented contact requests                   | No pressure, misleading claims, or unsolicited outreach |

## 8. Delivery phases

**Phase 0 — Foundation:** governance, policy mapping, IA, content models, design tokens.  
**Phase 1 — Minimum useful site:** homepage, legal pages, guide templates, category pages, contact flow, search basics.  
**Phase 2 — Content system:** editorial workflow, structured FAQ/recipe pages, schema validation, internal linking.  
**Phase 3 — Tools and scale:** wellness tools, localization, analytics governance, content expansion.

## 9. Required decisions before public launch

- Operating country/market and governing policy versions.
- Legal business name, independent-distributor details, and mandatory disclosures.
- Approved brand/trademark and image-use rules.
- Privacy notice, cookie policy, retention schedule, and consent wording.
- Approved claim library and review owners.
- Hosting, analytics, accessibility, security, and incident-response choices.

## 10. Governance

Every public page requires a named content owner, a source/approval record, a reviewer, and a review date. High-risk pages—health, product, earnings, testimonial, business opportunity, promotions, and data collection—require documented compliance approval before release.
