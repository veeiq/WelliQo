# WelliQo Compliance Manual

**Status:** Conservative working baseline — not legal advice and not a substitute for current Herbalife policies, local law, or written market approval.  
**Release requirement:** Confirm this manual against the current policy documents for each operating market before publishing.

## 1. Mandatory identity and transparency

- State the legal operator and a usable contact method on the site.
- If operated by an independent distributor, identify that status plainly wherever required; do not imply corporate affiliation.
- Use trademarks, logos, product names, images, marketing copy, and domain/social naming only with documented permission and according to current brand rules.
- Display the applicable privacy, cookie, terms, and any required product or earnings disclosures before data collection or commercial interaction.

## 2. Prohibited claims

Never publish or imply that products, routines, coaching, or joining the business will:

- diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or mitigate a disease or medical condition;
- guarantee weight loss/gain, athletic performance, health outcomes, or any individual result;
- guarantee earnings, rank, customers, lifestyle, travel, or financial independence;
- be suitable for every person, pregnancy, child, medical condition, allergy, medication, or dietary need;
- replace medical advice, medication, a balanced diet, or professional care.

Avoid transformations and testimonials that imply typical results or make outcome claims unless specifically approved, substantiated, properly qualified, and permitted in the market.

## 3. Product content rules

- Publish only current, market-approved product facts and approved wording.
- Do not create dosage, medical, treatment, or personalized nutrition instructions.
- Include preparation, allergen, nutrition, and safety information only from authorized sources and link/attribute as required.
- Do not display price, discount, availability, bundles, promotions, or purchasing mechanisms unless expressly authorized for that market and channel.
- Never use unapproved packaging images, comparative claims, user-generated product claims, or altered labels.

## 4. Business-opportunity content rules

- Describe the opportunity factually, with no income/lifestyle promises or selective success narratives.
- Do not recruit through deceptive job ads, investment language, urgency, or claims of easy/passive income.
- Use only approved earnings disclosures and approved opportunity materials.
- Clearly distinguish personal experience from typical experience where permitted, and never use personal experience to imply a likely financial result.

## 5. Leads, contact, and privacy

- Ask only for data needed for the stated purpose.
- Make consent language specific: what contact is requested, by whom, through which channels, and how to withdraw consent.
- Do not pre-tick marketing consent or condition educational content on marketing consent unless legally valid.
- Record consent, honor opt-outs promptly, secure data, restrict access, and retain it only under the published schedule.
- Provide a route for privacy requests and use safeguards required by applicable data-protection law.

## 6. Content review gates

| Content type                             | Required review                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| General wellness guide                   | Editorial + source check         |
| Product page, product mention, image     | Compliance/brand approval        |
| Weight-management content                | Compliance + health-claim review |
| Testimonial, before/after, success story | Compliance/legal approval        |
| Business opportunity or earnings         | Compliance/legal approval        |
| Lead form, quiz, tracking                | Privacy + compliance review      |
| Promotion, price, purchase link          | Market/channel approval          |

## 7. Red-flag words and patterns

Block or escalate phrases such as: “cures”, “treats”, “reverses”, “guaranteed”, “lose X kg”, “works for everyone”, “earn”, “financial freedom”, “quit your job”, “passive income”, “proven results”, and before/after transformations. Context can matter, but these terms always require review.

## 8. Publication checklist

- [ ] Audience and page purpose are clear.
- [ ] All statements are sourced and approved for the market.
- [ ] Required identity and disclosures are present.
- [ ] No medical, outcome, earnings, lifestyle, or misleading comparative claim appears.
- [ ] Images and trademarks have usage approval.
- [ ] CTAs are truthful and consent-based.
- [ ] Links, schema, metadata, and alt text match the page content.
- [ ] Reviewer and next review date are recorded.

## 9. Escalation

Do not publish when an approval, policy interpretation, claim substantiation, local-law decision, or asset license is unclear. Mark the item `blocked — compliance review` and request written confirmation from the authorized business/compliance owner.
