# Future Improvements

## Sequencing rule

Do not add fields or features because they seem useful. Each proposal needs a problem statement, user research, market/legal review, privacy impact assessment, ADR, rule/content ownership, tests, and rollback plan.

## Near-term after validated v1

- Additional locale and language packs with independently reviewed copy/rules.
- More goal routes and content-tag depth.
- Visitor-selected report saving and consented follow-up.
- Associate-facing sharing links that preserve disclosure and do not expose a visitor’s answers.
- Coach directory matching based on explicit preferences, never inferred traits.
- Rule authoring/review console with simulation fixtures and approval workflow.

## Later possibilities

- Accessibility personalization such as simplified-reading mode.
- Evidence and source explorer attached to recommendation blocks.
- Habit check-ins with voluntary reminders and clear retention controls.
- Aggregate, privacy-preserving content-gap insights.
- Carefully governed body-measure context only if validated, approved, and non-diagnostic.

## Explicit non-goals without a new approved design

- Live generative AI advice or chat.
- Medical triage, diagnosis, treatment plans, or supplement/product prescriptions.
- Income/business recommendations within the wellness route.
- Selling, pricing, checkout, pressure tactics, or contact gating.
- Behavioral profiling, ad targeting based on answers, or undisclosed data sharing.

## Continuous improvement

Maintain a test cohort of synthetic sessions covering every branch. Review completion, usefulness, accessibility, rule conflict, resource freshness, and consent quality—not body or health outcomes. A change is successful only if it improves usefulness without weakening safety, privacy, or informed choice.
