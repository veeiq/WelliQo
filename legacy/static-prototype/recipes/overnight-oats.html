Recipe

Simple overnight oats

A flexible make-ahead breakfast idea using familiar ingredients.

Prep: 10 minutes
Rest: Overnight
Serves: 1
Ingredient note: Check all ingredients for allergies, preferences, and individual dietary needs. This recipe provides general preparation ideas only.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup milk or a preferred alternative
  • 2 tablespoons yoghurt or a preferred alternative (optional)
  • Fruit, seeds, nuts, or spices to taste

Method

  1. Combine the oats and liquid in a clean, covered container.
  2. Stir in optional yoghurt and any chosen additions.
  3. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  4. Stir before eating and add fruit or other toppings as you like.

Make it your own

Use flavours and textures you enjoy. If you are unsure whether an ingredient is right for your situation, seek advice from an appropriate qualified professional.
