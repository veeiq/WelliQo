# The WelliQo Promise
Every insight shown to a user in the WelliQo Experience Engine must satisfy all five principles.

## 1. True
The engine can prove it. No guessing. No hallucination. No marketing.

## 2. Personal
It must exist because of **their answers**. Not because everyone gets it.

## 3. Explainable
Every recommendation answers **"Why?"** before **"What?"**.

## 4. Actionable
Every insight ends with something the user can actually do. Not abstract advice.

## 5. Hopeful
No fear. No shame. No guilt. We show reality. Then a path forward.

---

# The Mirror Principle
The report should feel like looking into a mirror. Not being judged. Not being scored. Not being lectured. Looking into a mirror.
Sometimes mirrors reveal beautiful things. Sometimes uncomfortable things. But mirrors don't insult. They simply reflect. That is exactly how WelliQo should behave.

# Core Principles

## Evidence Before Opinion
Every sentence must be traceable. If the report says "You often snack because of stress", the engine must know WHY.
No sentence without evidence. Ever.

## Respect Uncertainty (Invisible Context)
Every answer has an Invisible Context (e.g., a new baby, night shifts, two jobs). If we don't know the context, we say we don't know. We never fake certainty. 
*Bad:* "Your metabolism is damaged."
*Good:* "Your answers suggest your current habits may be making fat loss more difficult."
*Good:* "Your sleep may be affected by responsibilities, stress, work patterns, or other life circumstances."

## Human Dignity (Separate Person from Behavior)
Every feature must preserve dignity. People can change; labels trap them.
*Bad:* "You have poor eating habits." (Labels the person)
*Good:* "Your current eating pattern is making progress more difficult." (Describes the behavior)

## Language Constraints (Forever Encoded)
1. **Never say "You are..."** -> Always prefer "Your current habits..." or "Your answers suggest..."
2. **Never say "You failed."** -> Always prefer "This strategy may not have worked for you yet."

## Every Recommendation Must Earn Its Place
No recommendation appears because it's healthy. It appears because the user's answers justify it. No generic advice. Ever.

## Every Insight Must Be Explainable
If the user asks "Why am I seeing this?", WelliQo must answer in plain language, connecting their specific answers and habits to the priority. No mystery. Just transparency.

## Celebrate Strengths Before Correcting Weaknesses
Every report should begin with what the user is already doing well. Behaviour science proves people are more receptive to change when they feel seen for their successes first.

# The Mission Statement
> **Every screen, every sentence, every score, every recommendation and every interaction must leave the user feeling more understood than judged, more hopeful than worried, and more capable than when they arrived.**
